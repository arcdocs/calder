# How to get support

There are several ways to get help with using Aire and Calder. Please check this documentation and the [FAQ](../getting_started/faq.md) first, as your question may already be answered.

Choose the most appropriate contact route for your enquiry:

- Discuss HPC topics and share and explpre ideas: [Calder HPC Community](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/channel/19%3A86e20e375a044fa7964cf92512c06d4d%40thread.tacv2/Calder%20HPC%20Community?groupId=1ff8cb55-a479-4202-9dc4-a97891c03f42&tenantId=bdeaeda8-c81d-45ce-863e-5232a535b7cb)
- Ask for advice or request a feature or functionality: [Research IT Query](https://bit.ly/arc-help)
- Request a change to your storage quota: [HPC Quota Increase form](https://leeds.service-now.com/it?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=ca435a961bd5aa1063cf6467b04bcbe6&table=sc_cat_item&searchTerm=HPC%20quota%20increase)
- Request installation of new software: [HPC Application Install](https://it.leeds.ac.uk/it?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=48d5a6d70f275f00a82247ece1050ea0)
- Report a system fault or something that is not working: [Open an incident](https://leeds.service-now.com/it?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=3f1dd0320a0a0b99000a53f7604a2ef9)
- Connect with other HPC users: [Community HPC User Group (CHUG)](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/team/19%3ABCTnxgRmElgItpO_UU9fWoxAb4_vqvWmYxC1yTBJlj41%40thread.tacv2/conversations?groupId=fc043266-6de8-4044-939d-98e713d138f0&tenantId=bdeaeda8-c81d-45ce-863e-5232a535b7cb)

## Research Software Engineering (RSE)

The Research Software Engineering (RSE) team supports research projects with significant computational or software development requirements. The team can advise on project planning and computational requirements.

For more substantial software and workflow development, including programming reviews, performance analysis, code modernisation and refactoring, support is available through the RSE team's **Consulting Service**. Please use the appropriate route to enquire about this service rather than assuming that this work is part of general HPC support.

Find out more about the team and its work on the [Research Computing website](https://arc.leeds.ac.uk/).

We also welcome contributions from researchers who would like to share their experiences and work with the wider community. You could send us a testimonial, a blog post or a case study about, for example:

- A workflow or technique that you think could be useful to other HPC users.
- Benchmarks or performance improvements you have achieved.
- A paper you have published using the University's research computing systems.
- Feedback about the systems or your experience of using them.

We would be happy to help give your work greater visibility and show how researchers are using our systems. If you are interested in contributing, please email [RCTeam@leeds.ac.uk](mailto:RCTeam@leeds.ac.uk).

## Research Infrastructure Engineering (RIE)

The Research Infrastructure Engineering (RIE) team manages the infrastructure that supports research computing at the University. This includes the HPC service, LASER and General Compute. The team maintains and develops the underlying platforms, storage, networking and supporting services that enable researchers to run computational workloads reliably and securely.

For faults or service issues, please use the **Open an incident** route above so that the issue can be logged and tracked.

## Community HPC User Group (CHUG)

The Community HPC User Group (CHUG) brings together people who use the University's HPC systems. It provides an opportunity to meet other users, exchange experience, discuss common challenges and share ideas.

CHUG holds monthly meetings. These are open to every HPC user, and provide a forum for users to discuss their work and hear from others. Join the [Community HPC User Group on Microsoft Teams](https://teams.microsoft.com/l/team/19%3ABCTnxgRmElgItpO_UU9fWoxAb4_vqvWmYxC1yTBJlj41%40thread.tacv2/conversations?groupId=fc043266-6de8-4044-939d-98e713d138f0&tenantId=bdeaeda8-c81d-45ce-863e-5232a535b7cb) for community information and meeting updates.

## Email

Please use the contact routes listed above wherever possible. They help ensure that requests are directed to the right team and can be recorded and tracked where appropriate.

If your enquiry does not fit any of these categories, or you are unsure which route to use, you can email [RCTeam@leeds.ac.uk](mailto:RCTeam@leeds.ac.uk). The RSE and RIE teams share this address, and your enquiry will be directed to the appropriate team.
